# Story: June and the jar

## Narration
1. At sixty-two, June couldn't open a jar of pickles.
2. She blamed the lid.
3. You see, after forty, your muscles quietly start to disappear.
4. Up to eight percent, every single decade.
5. Nobody notices at first.
6. The shopping bags just feel a little heavier on the walk home.
7. The stairs feel a little steeper, and getting up from the sofa needs a push.
8. Then one night, June slipped on the landing, and her legs simply weren't there to catch her.
9. Three weeks on the sofa, waiting for a bruised hip to heal.
10. Her doctor wrote one prescription, and it wasn't a pill.
11. Lift something heavy. Twice a week.
12. So June walked into a gym full of people half her age and picked up the smallest dumbbell in the room.
13. Two kilos.
14. She could barely lift it, and the young man at the rack tried very hard not to stare.
15. But muscle never stops listening.
16. Week by week, it started to rebuild, the same way it does at twenty, just slower.
17. Four kilos. Then six. Then eight.
18. The young man started asking her for tips.
19. The shopping bags got lighter.
20. The stairs got shorter, and she started taking them two at a time.
21. By summer she was jogging through the park at sunset, past people half her age, walking.
22. And the jar of pickles?
23. Her son, thirty-five, couldn't open it.
24. June did. First try.
